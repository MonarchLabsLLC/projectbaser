The hand-off runs itself

Won deals open their own onboarding card.

Mark a deal won in PipeLeads CRM and the workflow tells the owner and drops an onboarding card into Backlog. You build it on a visual canvas, from 27 triggers and steps like Create card, Move card, Assign owner, Send email, Send a text, If/else and Wait.

  • ✓“Card due date approaching” warns the right person days before a deadline slips.
  • ✓Test run walks every step with writes switched off, so you see the result before anyone gets a message.
  • ✓Runs show each step, and a failed run retries from the step that failed. A workflow that keeps failing pauses itself and tells you.

Ask what’s late. Approve what changes.

Ask “What’s overdue on my boards?” and the assistant answers with the cards, each one linked. Ask it to create a card, move one, or leave a comment, and it prepares the change as an approval that shows exactly what will happen.

  • ✓Describe a workflow in one sentence. It arrives as a switched-off draft you open in the builder.

  • ✓Open it from the Agent button beside the card you’re on, or from Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor.

Straight answers

What ProjectBaser does.

The new ProjectBaser, stated plainly: what the assistant can touch, what workflows do, and what happens to your old boards.

Where does the new ProjectBaser live?

Inside PipeLeads Suite at go.pipeleads.ai, next to PipeLeads CRM. Sign in with your ScalePlus account and open Boards. The Sign in and Open ProjectBaser buttons on this page take you there.

Can the assistant change my boards on its own?

No. It reads boards and cards and answers with links. Every card it would create, move, or comment on becomes an approval you accept or reject, and an approval expires after 30 minutes. The assistant needs the Pro Max plan.

Do workflows run by themselves?

Only after a person switches them on. A workflow the assistant drafts arrives switched off. Test run shows what every step would do with writes turned off, and each run keeps a step-by-step history.

What can a card hold?

Members, labels, a due date, a description, checklists, custom fields, file attachments, comments with replies, booked meetings, and a history of changes.

What happens to my ProjectBaser 1.0 boards?

An owner or admin can import them. Boards, cards in order with due dates, descriptions, checklists, comments, and labels come across. Saved table and gallery views, board templates, and files stay behind.

Can I use it from Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor?

Yes. Through ClickCampaigns Superpowers, your coding assistant can list boards, read cards, find due work, and prepare card changes. The same approval step applies.
