Straight answers

What ProjectBaser does.

The current board workflow, stated plainly: what changes between views, what collaborators can do, and which decisions remain with your team.

Do the four views create separate copies of a project?

No. Kanban, Table, Gallery, and Calendar are different views over the same board data. Update a card and the underlying item remains connected across the views.

Can I add fields that match my workflow?

Yes. Cards support custom properties alongside common project details such as status, priority, assignee, tags, and dates.

Can teammates discuss work inside the board?

Yes. Add comments to cards, use @mentions, and share the board with view-only, edit, or admin access according to the collaboration needed.

Do I have to start every board from scratch?

No. Start from a pre-built template, create an empty board, or turn a useful board into a custom template for future work.

Can I move project data out of the board?

ProjectBaser includes export and backup workflows. Review the current options inside the board menu and choose the one that fits your operational need.

Who controls the project structure and changes?

Your team creates and controls the boards, cards, properties, comments, permissions, and templates. ProjectBaser renders the selected views and synchronizes collaborator changes.
